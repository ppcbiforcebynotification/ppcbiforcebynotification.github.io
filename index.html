Application information

Gard PPC Analytics

An internally operated analytics and workflow tool for authorized Google Ads accounts. It supports campaign performance reporting, conversion measurement and monitoring of advertising operations.

Advertising analytics

Organizes authorized Google Ads performance data to support campaign reporting and informed optimization decisions.

Lead-quality measurement

Helps compare advertising activity with conversion and lead-qualification outcomes supplied through authorized business systems.

Operational monitoring

Supports routine checks and diagnostic reporting for authorized advertising and conversion-data workflows.

Controlled access

This tool is intended for its authorized operators, not for public self-service registration. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Data and privacy

Access to Google Ads and related business information is limited to accounts and systems for which the operator has authorization. The application may process advertising reports, conversion identifiers and business-provided lead qualification data for analytics and account operations.

See our Privacy Policy for details about data categories, use, storage, disclosure and requests.
