Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy describes how Gard PPC Analytics, an independently operated internal advertising analytics tool (the “Tool”), handles information in the course of authorized Google Ads reporting, conversion measurement and operational monitoring. Contact: ppcbiforceby.manager@gmail.com.

Who uses the Tool

The Tool is designed for authorized advertising-account operators. This public informational website does not provide account registration or a public Google sign-in flow. Connecting an advertising account or business-data source requires authorization from the relevant account owner or administrator.

Information processed

Depending on the specific authorized workflow, the Tool may process:

  • Google Ads account, campaign, ad group, keyword and performance information, including spend and conversion statistics.
  • Advertising measurement identifiers and conversion-event metadata used for attribution and reporting.
  • Lead and conversion qualification information supplied by authorized business systems. These records may contain personal information, depending on the connected source.
  • Technical operational information, such as synchronization times, diagnostic results and execution logs.

How information is used

Information is used to report campaign performance, reconcile authorized business conversions, identify data-quality issues, monitor operational systems and support decisions by authorized advertising-account managers. Information is not provided for public browsing and is not sold to data brokers.

Storage, access and disclosure

Operational data is handled in access-controlled systems configured for the relevant advertising account. These may include Google Cloud, Google Workspace services such as Sheets and Drive, and private source-code/automation systems such as GitHub. Access is restricted to the authorized operator and other parties permitted for the applicable business workflow. Hosting and platform providers may process technical or operational information as required to deliver their services.

The public GitHub Pages website itself contains informational pages only. It does not include an account sign-in form, lead submission form, or our own analytics or advertising-tracking scripts. GitHub may process website access data under GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Operational records are retained according to the needs and controls of the authorized business workflow, including reporting, diagnostics and relevant legal obligations. Authorized account owners or affected individuals may contact us to ask about their information, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. Requests may require verification of identity or authority, and some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate operational obligations.

Google user data

Access to data obtained from Google services is limited to authorized API permissions and purposes described in this policy. If the Tool adds an interactive Google OAuth connection or requests new types of Google user information, this policy will be updated before that functionality is made available.

Updates and questions

We may update this policy when functionality, data processing or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published here with an updated date. Questions or requests may be sent to ppcbiforceby.manager@gmail.com.
